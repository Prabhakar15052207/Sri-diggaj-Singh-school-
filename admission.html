Admissions

Welcome to Sri Diggaj Singh Higher Secondary School, Kalindipuram. We welcome students from Nursery to Class 12.

Admission Enquiry

Parents and guardians can visit the school office to enquire about admission, available classes and the admission process.

Classes Offered

  • Nursery to UKG
  • Class 1 to Class 5
  • Class 6 to Class 8
  • Class 9 to Class 10
  • Class 11 to Class 12

Medium of Education

Hindi Medium and English Medium classes are available with separate classes and teachers.

Important Information

Admission dates, required documents, seat availability and fee details should be confirmed directly with the school office.
